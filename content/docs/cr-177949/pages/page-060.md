7.2 533 REPORT

The 533 report is a standardized contractor report used by the government
to monitor management contracts. It displays dollar and hourly summaries
according to Fixed Price Incentive, FPl, and Cost Plus Fixed Fee,
CPFF, tasks. This report can generate one of four quarterly reports based on
the current status date. The following status months produce quarterly
reports:

September - 1st quarter
December - 2nd quarter

```text
March     - 3rd quarter
June      - 4th quarter
```
