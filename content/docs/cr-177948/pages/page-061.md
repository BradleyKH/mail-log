The searches may also be assigned for a particular time frame. If

the data base and the last valid date defaults to 12-31-99. Of course, the

retrieve a month's or an entire year's worth of data by entering 00. For
example, entering 110078 would result in finding all the documents within the
data base In the eleventh month, November, for the year 1978. Likewise, an

entry of 000078 would retrieve all documents for the year 1978.
