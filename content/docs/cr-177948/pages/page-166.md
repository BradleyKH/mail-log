White the Search or Report is being compiled, a 'STAND BY' message
wlll be cisplayed and then the information will be displayed on the terminal
screen. If the report is longer than the terminal screen, a prompt 'MORE?'

‘OUTPUT TO PRINTER (Y OR N) >'. An 'N' response will end the session.
