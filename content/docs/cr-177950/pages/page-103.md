Option 5 generates a report on the Haloe Mechanical Parts file. The
report may Include only a specific category or all categories, and may be
sorted by Material Supply, by part number, or may remain unsorted. Only
printer output is available for the report. After the report has been
generated, the data base will be resorted back to Its original form, and the
user will be returned to the Report menu. Appendix C-4 contains an example
of the Special Materials Listing report output format. Refer to the fol low-

ing example.
