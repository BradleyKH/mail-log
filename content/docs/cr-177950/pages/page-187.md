All searches retrieve records that meet certain criterla and display
them at the terminal screen with the option to spool! the report for printer
output. When the search has been completed, the user will be returned to
the Search menu.

Options 1, 2, 3, 4, and 10, the Title, Company, Last Name, State, and
Citizenship searches all are partial searches and operate similarly. For
example, when performing a Company search, a search on the string 'NG' would
retrieve those records with a Company of 'BOEING' and 'HANGAR DIVISION'.
