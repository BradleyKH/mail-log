Option 2 performs the Title search which is a partial search. The
user enters a word to search on, such as 'BOLT', and those titles containing
the desired word will be displayed at the terminal screen. A search on
'BOLT' would retrieve such titles as 'BOLT CHECK’ and 'BOLT INVENTORIES'®.
The user may enter another search word to retrieve a single record or
smaller group of records from the titles displayed at the terminal screen.
When no more searching Is desired, the user must type 'QUIT' and then the
records that were retrieved will be displayed at the terminal screen in
their entirety. Printer output of thcse records is available. The user
will be returned to the Search menu after completing the search routine.
